# MAES17

> MAES17 is a geodesy, land surveying and land development (land management) practice operated by Erik Tovmasyan, individual entrepreneur. Contact: phone/WhatsApp/Telegram +374 41 171793. The website is available in Armenian (default), Russian and English.

MAES17 measures land and structures and prepares coordinates, plans and technical documentation for landowners, designers and builders. Prices and timeframes are not published; they are estimated per project. The geographic service area has not yet been published.

## Services

- [Geodesy and geodetic control networks]({{siteUrl}}/en/services/geodetic-control-networks/): reference points with known coordinates (GNSS and total station observations, network adjustment, coordinate lists).
- [Land surveying and dimensional measurements]({{siteUrl}}/en/services/land-surveying/): measured site plans of plots, buildings and features.
- [Topographic surveying and terrain mapping]({{siteUrl}}/en/services/topographic-surveying/): relief, spot heights, contours and site features; digital terrain models.
- [Cadastral surveying and property boundary determination]({{siteUrl}}/en/services/cadastral-surveying/): comparing title documents with the site and determining boundary points.
- [Construction surveying and setting-out]({{siteUrl}}/en/services/construction-surveying/): setting-out of axes and levels and control measurements during works.
- [Land management and land development]({{siteUrl}}/en/services/land-management/): plot division and layout plans and supporting documentation.
- [As-built surveys and technical documentation]({{siteUrl}}/en/services/as-built-surveys/): recording what was built and comparing it with design.
- [Engineering and specialised surveying]({{siteUrl}}/en/services/engineering-surveying/): profiles, cross-sections, volumes and monitoring.
